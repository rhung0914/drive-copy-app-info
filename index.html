Personal Drive Copy

A personal utility for copying Google Drive files between accounts controlled by its operator.

What the utility does

The utility copies accessible files and folders from a selected source Google Drive into a selected destination Google Drive. It can run on a daily schedule and preserve previous destination copies when files change.

It runs on the operator's computer. This website provides information about the utility and does not offer a sign-in form or an online file-transfer service.

Google permissions

  • The source connection requests read-only access to Drive metadata and file contents.
  • The destination connection requests access to files created or opened with this app, so the utility can create and maintain its own copies.

Google documents may be copied as portable exports. The utility does not copy source ownership, sharing permissions, comments or revision history.

Privacy policy and data handling
