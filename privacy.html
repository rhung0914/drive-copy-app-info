Personal Drive Copy

Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026.

Purpose and access

Personal Drive Copy is an operator-configured utility for copying files between Google Drive accounts. It uses Google authorization to read source files and metadata and to create or maintain destination copies within the granted permissions.

Data used

The utility accesses file contents to copy or export them. It uses metadata such as filenames, folder relationships, file identifiers, modification times, sizes and checksums to organize copies, identify changes and verify results.

Data flow and local storage

File data travels from the source Google Drive through the operator's computer to the selected destination Google Drive. Files may be buffered or temporarily stored locally during transfer. Authorization credentials and operational records are stored locally. Operational records may contain file metadata and copy outcomes.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used to perform the requested copies, verify them and maintain operational records. The utility does not sell file contents or use them to target advertisements. It does not grant other people access to source or destination files. Destination copies follow the access settings of the destination Google Drive.

The utility's use of Google user data is limited to its copy functionality and is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Destination copies and local operational records remain until the operator removes them. Temporary transfer files are removed when their transfer process closes. The operator can stop scheduled runs and revoke the utility's authorization in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not delete existing copies. The operator can manage or delete destination copies through Google Drive.
